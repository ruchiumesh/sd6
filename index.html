01 / THE BIG IDEA

Wastewater is not always the end of the story.

REWAVE explores the collection and treatment of relatively lightly contaminated wastewater from washbasins and showers for suitable non-drinking applications.

🚿

Collect locally

Capture suitable wastewater from hostel showers and washbasins using dedicated collection pipes.

🧪

Treat responsibly

Combine appropriate screening, filtration and any additional treatment or disinfection required by testing.

🌱

Reuse wisely

Supply only quality-approved water to selected non-potable uses such as irrigation and toilet flushing.

→ → → →

01. Collection

Dedicated plumbing collects suitable greywater from washbasins and showers while keeping it separate from sewage and drinking-water systems.

Important: Two cartridges and sensor lights do not, by themselves, prove water is safe. Treatment design and suitability for the intended use must be validated through appropriate water-quality testing.

03 / WHY REWAVE?

Big principles. Local implementation.

🏭

Large-scale treatment

Centralised infrastructure can serve large areas through extensive collection and distribution networks.

◦ Large treatment facilities

◦ Complex infrastructure in some settings

◦ Wide distribution networks

🏫

The REWAVE approach

Explore modular water reuse designed around a smaller, local collection and distribution network.

◦ College hostels and campuses

◦ Local residential communities

◦ Replaceable filtration components

We are not trying to reinvent water recycling. We want to explore how existing water-reuse principles could become practical and accessible at the scale of a hostel, campus or local community. Cost-effectiveness and easy maintenance are goals to validate through a pilot, not proven outcomes.

12 / FROM IDEA TO PILOT

Start small. Validate. Scale.

PHASE 01

Campus water audit

Measure actual washbasin and shower wastewater generation.

PHASE 02

System design

Determine flow, treatment, storage, plumbing and testing needs.

PHASE 03

Pilot installation

Build a small system for a selected hostel block or campus section.

PHASE 04

Testing & validation

Evaluate water quality, treatment performance, maintenance and costs.

PHASE 05

Controlled reuse

Start only approved non-potable uses after applicable requirements are met.

PHASE 06

Scale up

Expand based on measured results and demonstrated feasibility.
